## Brand & Style
This design system is engineered to project stability, intelligence, and clarity. The brand personality is that of a "Sophisticated Advisor"—someone who is composed, precise, and highly dependable. It targets individuals who value data-driven insights and require a secure environment for sensitive financial management.

The design style is **Corporate / Modern**. It leverages ample whitespace to reduce cognitive load while utilizing structured, high-quality typography to establish a clear information hierarchy. Every element is intentional, avoiding unnecessary ornamentation in favor of functional elegance. The interface prioritizes a sense of "digital calm," ensuring that even complex financial data feels manageable and safe.

## Colors
The color palette centers on **Trust Blue (#1E3A8A)**, a deep, authoritative navy that anchors the experience in reliability and institutional strength. **Success Green (#10B981)** is used purposefully for positive trend lines, completed actions, and growth indicators, providing a vibrant but professional contrast.

The neutral palette utilizes **Slate and Cool Grays** to maintain a crisp, clean environment. 
- **Primary:** Navigation, primary buttons, and active states.
- **Secondary:** Success states, profit margins, and safe-to-spend indicators.
- **Surface:** Pure white for cards and content areas to maximize contrast against the soft gray background.
- **Border:** Subtle dividers to provide structure without adding visual clutter.

## Typography
This design system utilizes **Inter** for all interfaces. It was chosen for its exceptional legibility in data-heavy environments and its neutral, systematic character.

- **Headlines:** Use Semi-Bold weights with slightly tightened letter spacing to create a sense of modern authority.
- **Body Text:** Standard weight is 400 for maximum readability. For financial figures, use a medium weight (500) to ensure they stand out as the primary focus of the page.
- **Labels:** Small, uppercase labels with increased tracking are used for categorizing data and section headers, providing a clear structural "skeleton" to the layouts.
- **Mobile Scaling:** Headlines larger than 24px should scale down by 15-20% on mobile devices to prevent excessive line-wrapping.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a **structured sidebar-and-content** model. This provides a distinct separation between navigation and the analytical workspace, reinforcing the feeling of an efficient dashboard tool.

- **Sidebar:** Fixed at 280px. It should have a slightly different background tone (e.g., a very light gray or the deep primary blue) to visually separate "Control" from "Data."
- **Grid:** A 12-column fluid grid system for the main content area. Gutters are set to 24px to ensure breathing room between financial cards.
- **Rhythm:** An 8px linear scale governs all spacing. Use 16px for internal card padding and 32px for section separation.
- **Responsive Behavior:** On tablet, the sidebar collapses into a hamburger menu or a slim icon-only bar. On mobile, the 12-column grid collapses into a single vertical stack with 16px side margins.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system is conveyed through **low-contrast outlines and ambient shadows**. We avoid heavy shadows to maintain a clean, professional aesthetic.

- **Base Level:** The main background is flat.
- **Level 1 (Cards/Containers):** Elements have a 1px border (#E2E8F0) and a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns/Modals):** Elements use a more pronounced shadow (0px 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)) to signify they are floating above the workspace.
- **Interaction:** On hover, interactive cards may slightly lift (shadow becomes more diffused) or change border color to Trust Blue.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Rounded**, using a consistent 8px (0.5rem) radius for standard components like buttons, input fields, and cards.

- **Small Components:** Tags and chips use a "Pill" shape (full rounding) to contrast against the more structured rectangular cards.
- **Large Containers:** Main content areas and modals use the standard 8px radius.
- **Visual Weight:** The medium rounding strikes a balance between the friendliness of a consumer app and the rigidity of a traditional banking portal.

## Components
Consistent component styling is vital for maintaining the "secure" feel of the system.

- **Buttons:**
  - **Primary:** Solid Trust Blue background with White text. Bold and high-contrast.
  - **Secondary:** Ghost style with a 1px Trust Blue border and Blue text.
- **Input Fields:** Use a 1px border. When focused, the border shifts to Trust Blue with a subtle 2px outer glow.
- **Cards:** White background, 1px border, and Level 1 shadow. Cards should always have a "Header" section for titles or key metrics.
- **AI Insights:** Specialized "Insight" cards use a very subtle gradient background or a Success Green left-accent border to denote AI-generated content.
- **Icons:** Outline style only. Use a 2px stroke weight. Icons should be monochrome (Slate Gray) unless they are active, in which case they adopt Trust Blue.
- **Charts:** Use Success Green for positive data and a soft Red (#EF4444) for negative data. Area charts should use light color fills with 10% opacity.
